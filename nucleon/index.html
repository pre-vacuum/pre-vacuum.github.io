The nucleon as topology and geometry

A separate, quantitative baryon program based on near-BPS/HLS/Skyrme structures. The goal is to predict a coherent set of nucleon observables from one action and one state rather than fit each observable independently.

Program

The nucleon track combines topological baryon structure with a common operator/Hessian treatment of static, electromagnetic, axial and rotational responses.

Current hard gates

  • common-Hessian and compactified-operator authority;
  • convergence and renormalization checks in the rotational sector;
  • full consistency of axial and transition currents;
  • closing remaining mass and response discrepancies without fitting the target observable.

Public standard

A “gold passport” is reserved for a state that passes topology, boundary conditions, residual, refinement and observable-response gates simultaneously.
