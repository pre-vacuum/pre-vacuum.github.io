Privacy Policy

How this research website handles analytics, contact information and your privacy choices.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. Site operator

This website is operated by Pavel Dubov. Contact: paveldubovmy@gmail.com.

2. Analytics is optional

By default, this site does not load Google Tag Manager or Google Analytics. They are loaded only after you choose to allow analytics in the privacy banner or Privacy settings.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may process information such as page views, referrer, browser/device information, general interaction data and an approximate location derived from network information. The site uses Google Tag Manager container GTM-KKDCS5BT and Google Analytics measurement ID G-MJL04KVWH3.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization are set to denied by the site's consent configuration.

3. Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored locally in your browser so that the site can remember it. You can change the choice at any time using in the footer. If analytics is disabled, the site also attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies created for this site.

4. Email contact

The website does not contain a contact form. If you email the address above, the message and your email address are processed by the relevant email providers in order to receive and answer your message.

5. External services and links

The site links to external research services such as GitHub and Zenodo. When you follow an external link, that service applies its own privacy practices.

6. Advertising and sale of data

This research site is not designed to serve targeted advertising or to sell personal information. Advertising consent signals remain disabled in the site's consent configuration.

7. Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights concerning personal data. For questions about information processed directly in connection with this site, contact paveldubovmy@gmail.com.

8. Changes

This notice may be updated when the site's services or analytics configuration changes. The date above identifies the current version.
