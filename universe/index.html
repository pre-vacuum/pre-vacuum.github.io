05 · Universe

Global geometry: when is the Universe finite?

This direction separates mathematical compactness theorems from observational statements about our Universe. Curvature hypotheses can force compactness; observations determine whether those hypotheses describe reality.

ACTIVE · THEOREM/OBSERVATION SEPARATEDEvidence & status
Mathematical toolPositive Ricci-curvature compactness bounds

Research goalConnect global geometry to parent dynamics

Two different statements

Conditional theorem: suitable positive lower curvature bounds imply compactness and diameter bounds. Physical claim: our spatial Universe satisfies those hypotheses. The second statement requires observational and dynamical justification.

Program

Combine topology searches, curvature constraints and cosmological observations without treating a theorem under assumed curvature as direct evidence for the actual global topology.
