02 · Vacuum & Defects

From a first distinction to a physical vacuum

This direction studies what happens after a nontrivial local structure exists: whether a stable vacuum, propagating excitations and localized defects can arise and survive continuum/refinement tests.

ACTIVE · NUMERICAL EVIDENCEEvidence & status
Strongest checkpointOpposite-sign localized structures in a scalar-model wave test
StatusModel-dependent numerical pass

Key constraintNear-exact Lorentz invariance at observable scales

Positive numerical result

In a preregistered scalar-model test, a global wave produced localized opposite-phase structures that survived grid refinement and an independent numerical scheme. This is evidence inside that model, not yet a derivation of the physical vacuum.

Why the old cell picture was downgraded

A literal fixed spatial foam or lattice faces severe propagation constraints. The current program therefore treats discretization as a computational representation unless a physical cell structure survives refinement and Lorentz-invariance tests.

Next gate

Derive the same defect-production mechanism from the parent pre-vacuum law and close the topology/current budget without inserting the localized objects into initial data.
