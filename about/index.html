About the project

A public notebook with hard status labels

Pre-Vacuum is the public-facing research atlas of Pavel Dubov's work across pre-geometric physics, vacuum dynamics, baryons, particle structure, global geometry and related mathematics.

Research practice

The project emphasizes reproducible calculations, explicit failure gates and “one object — one parameter set” where quantitative predictions are attempted. Hypotheses, numerical checks and established mathematics are labeled separately.
