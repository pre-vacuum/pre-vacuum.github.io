01 · Pre-Vacuum

Before spacetime: what can generate an event?

This branch asks for the smallest parent structure from which a local distinction, an ordering of events and eventually a physical vacuum could emerge—without assuming spacetime or particles at the start.

ACTIVE · PRIMARY GAP OPENEvidence & status
TargetPrimary localized distinction
Input disciplineNo pre-existing spacetime or particles
Current riskDynamics may be postulated rather than derived
Publication statusDraft / research program

Core question

What is the minimal law that turns an admissible difference into an actual event? Symmetry, combinatorics and state compatibility are useful constraints, but they do not by themselves supply real dynamics.

What this branch keeps separate

  • mathematical consistency versus physical realization;
  • existence of allowed configurations versus a law that generates histories;
  • a primary distinction versus later topological defects inside an already-formed vacuum.

Current program

Search for a parent rule that survives refinement and does not hide a continuum metric, a particle field or a fitted physical scale in its initial definition.
