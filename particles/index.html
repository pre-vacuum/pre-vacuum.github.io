04 · Particles

Electron and generations: derive, do not label

This branch asks whether fermion-like excitations, the electron and three generations can emerge from the same parent architecture without inserting their masses and family structure by hand.

OPEN · SEVERAL BRANCHES FAILEDEvidence & status
Primary targetElectron from a parent defect/vacuum law
Generation targetThree families from structure, not fit
Known issueSeveral symmetry/Hessian branches do not yield the required spectrum
RuleFailed branches remain visible

Why this page is mostly OPEN

There are mathematically interesting CP²/SU(2) constructions and cohomological multiplicities, but a multiplicity of modes is not yet a derivation of the observed charged-lepton spectrum.

Current requirement

A viable branch must produce the electron and family structure while also supplying the correct dynamics, charges, mass generation and stability. Merely obtaining “three” from a vector-space dimension is insufficient.

What is excluded

Branches that fail their own Hessian, BVP or spectrum gates are recorded as failed rather than recycled as evidence.
