Privacy

Cookie & storage Policy

What browser storage the site uses, and when optional analytics cookies may be created.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Necessary local storage

The site stores your privacy choice in your browser under pv_analytics_consent_v1. This is used only to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics.

Google Analytics cookies

Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are not loaded before analytics consent. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may create cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish sessions and visitors and to produce site-usage statistics.

Advertising cookies

The site's consent configuration keeps ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization set to denied.

Change or withdraw consent

You can reopen at any time. Rejecting analytics updates the consent state and the site attempts to remove its Google Analytics cookies.

Third-party websites

GitHub, Zenodo and other websites linked from this research atlas may use their own cookies after you leave this site. Their policies are separate from this one.
