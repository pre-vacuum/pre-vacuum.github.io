Rigidity, wreath products and scale invariance

A supporting mathematical program developed alongside the physics. It includes finite-index rigidity and co-Hopfianity questions for wreath products and related groups.

ARCHIVE + ACTIVE PAPERSEvidence & status

Paper track

  • finite-index rigidity over abelian and non-abelian bases;
  • finite lamps and index obstructions;
  • transfer of finite co-Hopfianity under group-theoretic hypotheses;
  • rank/dimension effects in abelian-by-cyclic groups;
  • strong scale-invariance and the search for structural obstructions.
