PREPRINT · 2026WREATH PRODUCTS

Stable Images of Finite-Index Self-Embeddings of Finite-Lamp Wreath Products

Studies finite-index self-embeddings of restricted wreath products with a finite lamp and the structure of their stable images. The emphasis is on rigidity: which self-similar finite-index images can persist under iteration, and which group-theoretic data obstruct them.

Pavel ChekhovDOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22772094

PREPRINT · 2026FINITE CO-HOPFIANITY

Finite Co-Hopfianity of Restricted Wreath Products over Torsion-Free Groups

Investigates when a restricted wreath product admits no proper finite-index copy of itself. The paper isolates torsion-free base hypotheses that turn finite-index self-embedding questions into a rigidity problem rather than a scale-symmetry construction.

Pavel ChekhovDOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22855916
PDF ↓Zenodo ↗
PREPRINT · 2026SYMMETRY / REALIZABILITY

Realizability of a Law’s Symmetry and the Limits of Forced Local Heterogeneity in Three-Role Root Models

Separates symmetry of a formal law from realizability of asymmetric local states. In the three-role root setting it identifies what symmetry can constrain and why symmetry alone does not automatically force the local heterogeneity one might want for a physical interpretation.

Pavel ChekhovDOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22861804

PREPRINT · 2026GROUP THEORY

Finite-Index Rigidity of Wreath Products over FL-Groups with Nonzero Euler Characteristic

Develops a finite-index rigidity result for wreath products over FL-groups with nonzero Euler characteristic. The core theme is transfer: structural information in the base group constrains proper finite-index self-embeddings of the wreath product.

Pavel ChekhovDOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22932859
